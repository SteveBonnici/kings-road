About the cottage

The cottage sits on our property, tucked away behind a secure gate. It's quiet and private, and it feels a world away from the city while still being close to everything.

The open-plan living area has polished timber floors, a fireplace and big windows that bring in lovely morning sun. The kitchen opens off the living space and has gas cooking. Both bedrooms have built-in wardrobes and leafy outlooks, and the bathroom is modern with a walk-in shower. Ranch sliders open onto a large private deck under a pergola, enclosed by high hedging and established gardens.
