About the cottage

The cottage sits on our property, tucked away behind a secure gate. It's quiet and private, and it feels a world away from the city while still being close to everything. It has just had a full repaint, inside and out.

The open-plan living area has polished timber floors, a fireplace and big windows that bring in lovely morning sun. The kitchen opens off the living space and has induction cooking and a new rangehood. Both bedrooms have built-in wardrobes and leafy outlooks, and the newly renovated bathroom has floor-to-ceiling tiles, a frameless glass shower and a wall-hung timber vanity. Ranch sliders open onto a large private deck under a pergola, enclosed by high hedging and established gardens.

It comes with a table and chairs, a fridge, a front-loader washing machine and a condenser dryer.
